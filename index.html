interactive photonics simulations

Small, self-contained simulations for teaching and exploring photonics, from undergraduate optics to research-level nonlinear cavity dynamics. Everything runs in your browser: no install, no server, and you can open any page offline.

Optical cavity: transmission & bistability

One- and two-port Fabry–Pérot and ring resonators in temporal coupled-mode theory, with thermo-optic (red shift) or free-carrier (blue shift) nonlinearity. Watch the laser follow the stable branch and the hysteresis appear.
