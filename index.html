interactive photonics simulations

Small, self-contained simulations for teaching and exploring photonics, from undergraduate optics to research-level nonlinear cavity dynamics. Everything runs in your browser: no install, no server, and you can open any page offline.

Optical cavity: transmission & bistability

One- and two-port Fabry–Pérot and ring resonators in temporal coupled-mode theory, with thermo-optic (red shift) or free-carrier (blue shift) nonlinearity. Watch the laser follow the stable branch and the hysteresis appear.

coupled-mode theoryoptical bistabilitythermal / FCD

Cavity optomechanics: transduction & backaction

Linearized optomechanics read out on an ESA. Transduction vs detuning in the resolved and unresolved sideband regimes, thermal bistability during laser sweeps, converting PSD (dBm) to displacement, optical spring and damping vs power, sideband cooling and OMIT.

dynamical backactionPSD calibrationOMIT

Loop taper on a waveguide: loss calibration

Enter the measured powers or losses of each component (circulator, splitters, suspended taper) and extract the per-pass fiber ↔ waveguide coupling and the scattering at the contact from the transmission drop and the reflection from an on-chip reflector.
