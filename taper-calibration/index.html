Model

T_t = suspended-taper transmission; T_a = T_t^f (before contact), T_b = T_t^(1−f) (after) R = reflector × waveguide propagation (round trip) Transmission: P(2) = P(1) · T_a · (1 − η − s) · T_b → τ = P(2) / (P(1)·T_t) = 1 − η − s Reflection: P(3) = P(1) · T_a · η · R · η · T_a · T_c23 + P_bg ρ = (P(3) − P_bg) / (P(1) · T_a² · T_c23) = η² · R Solution: η = √(ρ / R), s = 1 − τ − η

Assumptions: co-directional, reciprocal coupling (same η and s on the way in and on the way back); light returning in the waveguide that is not re-coupled continues to the far end of the waveguide and is lost (no cavity); background added incoherently; backscattering at the contact is included in the background. The uncertainty propagates an independent σ (dB) on every reading that enters the calculation. The monitor beam splitter and the polarization controller only affect the result if the power reference is the laser or the monitor.
