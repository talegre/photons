The laser moves at the finite rate dωl/dt and the full CMT equations are integrated in time, so the field relaxes onto whichever stable branch it is on and jumps only when that branch ends. Adiabatic following requires (dωl/dt)·τ ≪ κ and dωl/dt ≪ κ².
